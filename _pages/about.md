Hi! 👋 My name is Alex, and I’m a writer from the Netherlands. Every time I start a blog, I’m filled with inspiration for all the great stories I’m going to write. But as soon as my dopamine levels drop below zero after tinkering with CSS and HTML, I simply forget my blog even exists. Yet somehow, I keep trying. It’s a vicious circle.

However, if I DO write something, it’s probably about the things I love most: watching movies and TV shows, pretending I’m good at gaming, reading books, building LEGO, and all things tech.

If you want to reach out, you can always send me an email or say hi on Threads.
