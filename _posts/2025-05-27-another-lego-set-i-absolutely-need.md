I do not actually need more LEGO, because the unbuilt sets are literally piling up.

But every few weeks a new set appears that I simply MUST have. And today, here we go again...

## A New Category Of LEGO Sets

LEGO has been releasing sets for years in different lines I really love. Especially the Harry Potter and other pop culture sets are must-haves for me.

But since this year, they have also started releasing book nooks. Basically something you place between a row of books. They started with one based on Sherlock Holmes, and that one was pretty decent. But not "I MUST HAVE THIS!!" decent.

But [from today you can preorder a Lord of the Rings book nook](https://www.lego.com/nl-nl/product/the-lord-of-the-rings-balrog-book-nook-10367), featuring the famous scene of Gandalf versus the Balrog.

And yes, sorry, but that one simply has to come home.

## Take My Money!

You can preorder it now for EUR119.99, which is on the pricey side. The set has 1,201 pieces.

I hope LEGO releases more of these sets soon, because the money is already burning a hole in my pocket again.
