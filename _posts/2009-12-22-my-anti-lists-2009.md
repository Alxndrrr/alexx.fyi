The end of the year means list season in music land. Everywhere you see top-somethings of artists, albums and songs. Some are complete nonsense. Like the annually recurring Top 2000. For ten years, the same artists have been in the top five. They only flip a coin each year to decide who gets which spot, but otherwise it is always Queen, Boudewijn de Groot, The Eagles, Deep Purple and Led Zeppelin.

I also put together a few lists this year. Not necessarily based on albums released in 2009, but on music that gave me the best feeling this year. Music I have the best memories of. The best performances of the year, and a list of the biggest disappointments of the year.
