Original ideas have been missing in Hollywood for years, but television is increasingly trying to score easy points too.

Because now there is some kind of spin-off of _The Office (US)_, also known as [the best comedy series ever](https://www.alexx.fyi/the-ten-best-tv-shows-ever/). I am curious, but watching the trailer also makes me sceptical.

_The Paper_ is about the newsroom of an American newspaper that is struggling badly. The documentary crew from **The Office** will be documenting these events this time.

{% include youtube.html id="c5v4LJJkvUU" %}

When I watch **The Office**, I burst out laughing multiple times. Again and again, even though I have seen the entire series several times. But when I watch the trailer for **The Paper**, I can barely manage a smile.

Everyone who has seen **The Office** knows the strength of that show lies in its recognisable characters. Everyone works with someone who resembles one of them.

Of course it is too early to say, but I do not see that happening quickly with **The Paper**, at least not without it becoming too much of a watered-down version of the original.

And no, Oscar does not count.

Still, I am curious. **It will be available on Peacock from September**, which usually means SkyShowtime here in the Netherlands.
