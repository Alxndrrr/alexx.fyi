In [the nineties, many things were better](https://www.alexx.fyi/the-nineties-were-peak-cinema/), and that decade gave us a lot. One of the most beautiful things from the nineties is **Jurassic Park**, one of my favourite films.

Since then, five sequels have been released, but honestly, most of them were not very good. Okay, Chris Pratt was fun in **Jurassic World** from 2015, and that gave me hope for the sequels.

But no, the rest was bad.

{% include youtube.html id="jan5CFWs9ic" %}

Still, they are going to try again, and this year the sixth film will be released: **Jurassic World: Rebirth**.

And honestly?

The trailer gives me hope again. It feels much more like an adventure film than before.

So bring it on.

In cinemas on July 3, 2025.
