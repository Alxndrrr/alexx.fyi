Mobile-first design is not just about shrinking a desktop website. It starts with the most focused version of the page.

The small screen asks every element to earn its place. Navigation should be clear, typography should breathe, and the page should keep moving without feeling crowded.

This dummy post is here to show how paragraphs, headings, and categories look inside the theme.
