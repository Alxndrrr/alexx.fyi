A personal site becomes more useful when it is easy to maintain. The theme should make common edits obvious and keep repeated structure out of your way.

That is why this setup uses layouts, includes, and a single SCSS file with clear sections. You can change the visual identity without rewriting every page.

Good infrastructure is quiet. It lets the writing happen.
